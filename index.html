    How recording works

    1. Tap Record. If the page may use your microphone it records right here (tap again to stop). Otherwise your phone opens its own recorder, or lets you pick a sound file.
    2. The silence before the sound is cut off and the volume is levelled. Gun shots are also cut to the length on the slider and fade out at the end.
    3. Happy with it? Tap Send in. Everyone sees it under that sound with your name and can give it 1 to 5 stars (tap the same star again to take your stars back). You cannot rate your own.
    4. Send in a new one to replace your old entry for that sound. The best-rated entry for each sound goes into the game.
